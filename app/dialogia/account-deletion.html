Delete Your LaVerba Account and Data

LaVerba is published by Anastasiia Losieva and was formerly known as Dialogia.

Delete Your Account in the App

You can permanently delete your account directly in LaVerba:

  1. Open LaVerba.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Account & Privacy.
  4. Select Delete account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

Request Deletion Without the App

If you cannot access the app, send an account-deletion request to info@losieva.com.

Use the email address associated with your LaVerba account and include the words LaVerba account deletion request in the subject line. We may ask for additional information solely to verify that the account belongs to you. Requests are normally completed within 30 days after any required verification.

Data Deleted With Your Account

Deleting your account deletes the account and account-linked data controlled by LaVerba, including:

  • your Firebase Authentication account;
  • your account-linked user profile and consent record;
  • synced learning progress, synced AI-generated and imported dialogs, dialog edits, and dialog-sync metadata; and
  • account-linked AI and grammar usage records.

Data That May Remain

  • When deletion is completed in the app, account-scoped local data is also removed from that app installation. Data already present on another offline device cannot be remotely erased and may remain there until its app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled.
  • Speech-recognition quality reports submitted without an account identifier and shared grammar-cache records that are not linked to your account cannot be identified as account data.
  • Apple, Google Play, and RevenueCat may retain purchase, subscription, and entitlement records under their own legal, financial, security, and operational retention requirements.
  • Limited technical or security records may be retained only where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or reliable service operation and only for as long as required for those purposes.

Subscriptions

Deleting your LaVerba account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel your subscription separately through Google Play or the Apple App Store.
